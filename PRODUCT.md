# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Potential clients:** businesses, organisations and radio stations that want to know who Thomas is and what he does before getting in touch.
- **Blog readers:** people who arrive through search for practical radio/broadcast and web posts (for example mAirlist, audio routing and DNS). The landing page introduces the blog to them as well.

## Product Purpose
This is the personal business site of Thomas Mongstad. It says who he is, what the business does, and how to reach him, and it hosts his blog. Success means a visitor understands the offer and knows how to make contact without being pushed.

## Positioning
Consulting and programming from a person with hands-on depth in radio and broadcast automation, built on general web and IT work. Landscaping is a small side service and gets only a brief mention.

## Operating Context
- Built with Astro (the Hugo setup is being retired and is not a reference) and hosted on GitHub Pages at mongstaen.github.io.
- Bilingual: Norwegian (nb) is primary at `/` and English (en) is secondary at `/en/`.
- The landing page plan is tracked in GitHub issue #3.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Landing page: a short intro, what the business does (consulting and programming first, landscaping briefly), the three latest blog posts, and a calm contact line using email, with no form.
- No sales CTAs such as "Book et møte!". The tone is to inform, not sell.

## Brand Commitments
- Business name: **Thomas Mongstad**.
- Voice: informal and personal, written in first person.
- Norwegian is the primary language.
- Visual stance (chosen 2026-11-03): classic and calm, the category standard done well. The quality bar is rauno.me, paco.me and leerob.com: minimal, text-first and precise.
- Contact email: thomas@mongstad.dev

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing blog posts on radio automation, audio and networking.
- There are no testimonials, client list, pricing or case studies. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Inform, don't sell.
2. Be personal and honest. It's one person, so don't present the business as an agency.
3. Lead with real expertise: show radio/broadcast depth through the actual writing.
4. Norwegian first, with English as an equal-quality option.
